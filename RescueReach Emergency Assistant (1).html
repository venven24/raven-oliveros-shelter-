📍 Evacuation Preparedness & Safety Guidelines

Offline checklist and evacuation site protocols during major typhoon or flooding emergencies.

🎒 Go-Bag Emergency Checklist

  • 1 Gallon of Drinking Water per person (3-day supply)
  • Non-perishable food (Canned goods, biscuits)
  • First Aid Kit + Prescription medications
  • Waterproof pouch for legal ID, Cash, documents
  • Flashlight, whistle, battery/power banks

🏠 Evacuation Protocol Steps

  1. Switch off main circuit breaker (Electricity) & Gas lines.
  2. Elevate electronics, furniture, and valuables above flood line.
  3. Lock windows and doors securely before leaving.
  4. Wear sturdy waterproof footwear (avoid wading barefoot).
  5. Proceed immediately to designated Barangay Evacuation Center.
